    Bin Rocket Sky

    Deathmatch: 5-minute rounds, the pilot with the most kills wins. Scoop up sparks to level up, blast space junk and other bins with fire. You start at 100% health. Gold spark +5, junk +2, hit +1, knockout +5. Sparks level you up: a bigger bin, more health and fancier fire. Planets pull you in and hurt if you slam into them (more on boost); shoot one apart for +25. Planets, wrecks and buildings break off piece by piece where you hit them. Watch out for black holes, meteor showers and aliens. Grab weapon crates: triple shot, laser, homing rockets (slow: outrun them or shoot them down), scatter gun, railgun (pierces), mines, plasma orb (shoot it with the railgun or laser for a combo blast), lightning, shard cannon (shards ricochet), and the rare nuke. The violet Overdrive crystal triples your damage for 20 s (it levels everything around it). Medkits heal, shields soak damage. Black holes swallow anything that reaches the centre. Your bin has inertia: push the other way or hold X to brake. Modes for a new lobby: Deathmatch, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Nukes only and Railgun instagib.
