    Effects
    Glow and colour
    Light and shadows: fire, shots and lamps light the scene and cast shadows. Smoke: blasts and engines leave rolling smoke. Glow: bright things bleed light. Auto gives them up one at a time when the game runs slow (smoke first), not all at once.
    Mouse
    Aims: the gun follows the cursor, WASD flies, left button fires. Steers: hold the button to fly toward the pointer.

    Weapon controls

    Fixed slots: 1–9, 0; Alt+1–6 for additional weapons. Q/E cycles carried weapons. Hold Tab: scores.Gamepad: left stick flies, right stick aims, RT fires, LT second mode, A boosts (tap: dash), B brakes, LB/RB switch guns, Start: settings.
