    Bin Rocket Sky

    Deathmatch: 5-minute rounds, the pilot with the most kills wins. Scoop up sparks to level up, blast space junk and other bins with fire. You have 3 lives. Gold spark +5, junk +2, hit +1, knockout +5. Sparks level you up: a bigger bin, more lives and fancier fire. Planets pull you in and hurt if you slam into them (more on boost); shoot one apart for +25. Watch out for black holes, meteor showers and aliens. Grab weapon crates: triple shot, laser, rockets, scatter gun, railgun, mines, and the rare nuke (it levels everything around it). Medkits heal, shields soak damage. Black holes swallow anything that reaches the centre.
